Account Deletion

Delete your account

At Kisakye Delivery, we respect your privacy and provide full control over your personal data.

How to request account deletion

Method 1: In the App

Open Kisakye Delivery → Go to Profile / Settings → Tap Delete Account.

Method 2: Via Email

Send an email to mwesicloudsolutions@gmail.com with the subject line:

Account Deletion Request - [Your Phone Number or Email]

What data will be deleted?

  • Your profile details (Name, Phone Number, Email Address).
  • Saved delivery addresses and order history.
  • Authentication and account credentials.

What data is kept?

Financial and transaction records may be retained for up to 90 days where required for legal, accounting, and anti-fraud compliance.

Processing time

Account deletion requests submitted via email will be processed within 7 business days.
